PRICING

What “From” and “Fixed” mean

“From” prices use a site-cost allowance and can change after soil tests and site investigation. “Fixed” prices apply to that lot and design as listed. We re-confirm with the builder partner before anything is signed.

TITLE

Titled now vs expected titles

Titled-now lots can generally move to settlement sooner. Expected titles show an estimated month, but developer and council timing can shift. Allow buffer in your finance and rental plans.
