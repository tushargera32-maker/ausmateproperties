Finance guides · General information only

Borrow for a build without surprises.

House-and-land finance has more moving parts than an established purchase — land settlement, build stages, and site costs. General information only, not credit advice. Speak with your broker or bank about your borrowing position.

01 — START EARLY

Talk to a lender before you shortlist

Confirm your borrowing position, deposit and buffer first — then filter packages to what you can actually settle.

02 — COMPARE LIKE FOR LIKE

Fixed price vs “from” allowance

“From” prices use a site-cost allowance that can move after soil and site investigation. Ask what is fixed, what is provisional, and what triggers a variation.

03 — CONFIRM IN WRITING

Nothing is live until it is verified

Availability and prices change quickly. We confirm with the builder partner in writing before you pay anything or sign.

What to understand first

The concepts buyers ask about most — in plain terms.

Deposits you will see here

Some lots ask for a 5% land deposit or 10% deposit; others are titled now. Construction loans usually pay in stages against completed work. Confirm the deposit, who holds it, and when each payment falls due — with us and your lender.

Titled vs untitled

“Titled now” can settle sooner. “Expected title” dates mean the land settles later — ask your lender about rate holds, pre-approval expiry and valuation risk if values move before settlement.

What lenders check

Income, living expenses, deposit, savings pattern, credit file — plus the build contract and whether site costs are fixed or “from” allowances. Bring the builder flyer to your broker so the quote matches the real package.

Construction payments in stages

Build funds are typically drawn progressively (slab, frame, lock-up, fit-out, completion) with lender inspections along the way. Ask who pays interest during construction and how progress claims are approved.

Valuation risk to watch

Lenders value the land-plus-build on its merits — a valuation below the contract price can change your deposit or loan. Ask your broker what happens in that case before you commit, especially on untitled lots.

Questions for your broker

Is this pre-approval or conditional? How long does the rate hold last? What fees, LMI and buffers apply? What paperwork do you need from the builder? Written answers beat verbal ones.

General information only, not credit, financial or legal advice. Lending rules, rates and policies differ between lenders and change over time — confirm everything with your broker or bank for your situation.
