House and land packages

Grouped by corridor. Open any package for flyer, floor plan, lot details and inclusions.

“From” prices use a site-cost allowance and may change after site investigation. Prices exclude stamp duty, legal, bank and government charges unless stated. First home buyer prices are as shown on the builder's flyer and depend on eligibility. Availability changes quickly — call 0430 200 150.

What “standard inclusions” means

Every builder, design and promotion differs — this is a checklist for comparing flyers, not a promise. Always confirm against the flyer for your exact lot.

Often in the build price

Floor coverings · kitchen appliances · heating & cooling · energy-rating items · standard facade · internal paint

Often excluded / site-specific

Site costs & retaining · driveway · fencing & landscaping · letterbox · blinds · facade upgrades

Fixed vs “from”: fixed prices are as stated by the builder; “from” prices use a site-cost allowance and can move after soil and site investigation.
